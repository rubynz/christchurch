This month, Patrick Davey will give an overview of the evolution of dealing with spam on a personal website.  Watch this to get you in the mood for the talk -> https://youtu.be/mBcY3W5WgNU?t=8

> I've been running snowpool for a while, starting out when the Internet was a carefree idyll and morphing into what it is today.  In this talk I'll show the evolution from the zero spam-checking days to today and some thoughts about what I'd need to do next if the spam keeps on coming.  This is more of an extended lightning talk than a talk talk so there probably will be time for a couple of :zap: talks after :)
