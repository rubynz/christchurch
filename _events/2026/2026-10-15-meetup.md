For our October meetup, Rob Ingram is going to expand our horizons…

"These days we’re all used to generating code with AI but this month we’ll go one step further to explore the landscape of no-code tools, which let anyone build live, working apps without writing any code or, potentially, worrying about infrastructure. We’ll present a summary of a cross section of tools covering the principles behind each, what they do well and where they fall short.

Then, we’ll split into teams and use the tools to build an app in real time. A sample app will be provided, or bring your app idea or new side hustle! All of the tools that we’ll cover provide a free tier or trial period. We’ll also discuss some approaches to getting the most out of the limited build credits on some of the free tiers. Bring a laptop if you want to participate."
